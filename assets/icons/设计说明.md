# 项目手记图标

由内置 imagegen 生成，作为 Mac、Windows 及应用内的统一图标。

暖白纸页／陶瓷质感，森林绿、鼠尾草绿与少量陶土色的 4×4 时间方格。浅浮雕与柔和阴影，透明背景。

文件：
- 项目手记-原始.png：生成工具返回的原始透明图。
- 项目手记-1024.png：用于分发的 1024 像素图。
- 项目手记.ico：Windows 多尺寸图标，已嵌入可执行文件。
- AppIcon.iconset：Mac 多尺寸图标素材，应用中的 AppIcon.icns 已包含对应尺寸。

完整生成提示词：

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: a production macOS and Windows desktop app icon for a personal project-time journal named 项目手记. Create ONE polished original Apple-inspired productivity app icon, not an Apple logo and not a mockup. A precisely rounded squircle warm ivory ceramic/paper tile with a gentle dimensional edge and extremely soft realistic shadow, centered on a transparent 1024 square canvas, tile occupying about 85% canvas width. On the tile, a centered small calendar/commit-grid mark made of a disciplined 4 by 4 matrix of rounded squares, subtly inset or embossed; a few squares in forest green, sage and pale warm gray, one understated terracotta square. The mark communicates time accumulating over days and creative work. Symmetrical, optically balanced, exceptionally simple and recognizable at 32px, restrained macOS icon craft, soft top left light, tactile matte paper, refined shallow depth. Color palette ivory #f6f3eb, forest green #315c46, pale sage #b4cda4, warm clay #a2553e. No text, no letters, no numbers, no clock hands, no pencil, no Apple logo, no extra objects, no scenery, no icon sheet, no frame around the canvas. Real transparent pixels outside the tile and its subtle shadow. Deliver the icon asset alone.
